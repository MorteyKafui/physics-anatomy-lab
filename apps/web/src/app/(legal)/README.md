# Terms of Use and Privacy Policy (drafts)

`terms.md` and `privacy.md` are the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy tabs of the Legal and Policy
Pack (D25, ADR 0025; claude.ai/artifact/GUuasFC7PQhYAj67nFiNmA), copied word for word on
2026-11-25. Mortey asked for them to be published as drafts while UEW reviews them, so the
sign-in and join forms can link to them. The only change is "tab" to "document" where the text
points at another part of the pack.

`terms-open.md` and `privacy-open.md` are what the pages show while accounts are switched off
(ADR 0047): the same documents with the parts about accounts, classes, teachers and AI taken out,
and the privacy table saying that nothing is kept about a visitor except the anonymous daily
counts (ADR 0048). They are drafted here, not copied from the pack, so the pack needs the same
open-lab version added before UEW's review; until then these two are the record of it.

The pack is the master. When it changes, copy the change here; never edit only this copy. The
blanks in double brackets (`[[Contact email]]`) are left for the operator to fill, and the pages
show them highlighted. The version that goes live is set in `apps/api/app/legal.py`.
