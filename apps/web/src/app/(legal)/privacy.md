# Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 (draft) · Effective [[Effective date]] · For review by UEW's legal office and a data-protection practitioner

**The short version.**

- Most students never give us their name, email or age. Signed-out work stays on your device.
- We collect only what we need to run the lab, keep it safe, and improve it.
- We never sell data, never show ads, and never build marketing profiles.
- Analytics are off until you say yes.
- Our AI provider may not train its models on what you type.
- You can see, download and delete your data at any time.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

[[Operator]] is the data controller, registered with Ghana's Data Protection Commission under number [[DPC registration number]]. Our Data Protection Supervisor is [[Data Protection Supervisor]], reachable at [[Privacy email]].

## 2. What we collect, and why

| Who you are                                | What we collect                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Why                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Anyone, signed out                         | Your lab work, stored **only on your device**. A random device ID, also on your device. If you agree to analytics: anonymous usage events (pages, experiment steps, time on step, errors)                                              | To run the lab offline; analytics only with consent, to improve the lab            |
| Student in a class                         | A display name you choose (not your real name, if you prefer), your class, your attempts (readings, tables, graphs, answers, snapshots, reports) and the steps you took in the lab. **No email, phone number, date of birth or photo** | So your teacher can see and mark your work, and you can continue on another device |
| Teacher or lecturer                        | Name, email, school or institution, and your Google profile name and picture if you sign in with Google; your classes and assignments                                                                                                  | To run your account and classes                                                    |
| Anyone who asks for help or sends feedback | Your message, and the attempt it is about if you choose to attach it                                                                                                                                                                   | To answer you and fix problems                                                     |
| Anyone using AI features                   | The question or text you send, plus the experiment and step you are on. **We never send your name or email to the AI provider**                                                                                                        | To answer your question                                                            |
| Everyone                                   | IP address and browser type, processed briefly by our hosting provider for security and abuse prevention; error reports with personal data removed                                                                                     | To keep the Service safe and working                                               |

**What we do not collect:** your location, contacts, camera or photos, or recordings of your voice. Spoken coaching is played to you; nothing you say is recorded in this version.

## 3. Our lawful bases

- **Providing the Service you asked for:** accounts, classes, sync, reports.
- **Legitimate interests:** security, preventing abuse, and fixing errors, always balanced against your rights.
- **Consent:** analytics, research participation and, for students under 18, the school's or parent's permission described in the Children and Schools document. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect what happened before.
- **Legal obligation:** keeping records the law requires.

## 4. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers who process it for us under written contracts, and only as much as each needs:

| Provider                    | What they do for us                             | Data involved                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cloudflare                  | Hosting, security, file storage, email delivery | All data we hold passes through or is stored on their network                                                                               |
| Neon                        | Database hosting                                | Account, class and attempt data                                                                                                             |
| Anthropic                   | AI tutor, scenarios and report review           | The text of AI requests, without names or emails; Anthropic may not train on it ([terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms)) |
| [[Text-to-speech provider]] | Generating the coach's voice                    | Only our scripted lines and the AI tutor's replies, never your personal data                                                                |
| Sentry                      | Error reports                                   | Technical details, with personal data removed                                                                                               |
| PostHog                     | Analytics, only with your consent               | Pseudonymous usage events                                                                                                                   |

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect a child or anyone else from serious harm (see the Children and Schools document). We never sell personal data.

## 5. Data stored outside Ghana

Our providers store and process data in data centres outside Ghana, including in Europe and the United States. We use providers that commit to strong security and to contract terms that protect your data to the standard Ghanaian and European law expects.

## 6. How long we keep data

| Data                        | Kept for                                                                          |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Signed-out lab work         | On your device until you delete it; never on our servers                          |
| Student attempts in a class | While the class exists, then 12 months, then deleted (teachers can delete sooner) |
| Teacher accounts            | Until you delete the account, then 30 days in backups                             |
| Help requests               | 24 months                                                                         |
| AI request logs             | 30 days, then only anonymous counts                                               |
| Analytics events            | 24 months, pseudonymous                                                           |
| Security logs               | 90 days                                                                           |
| Consent records             | Life of the account + 6 years, as proof                                           |

## 7. How we protect it

Encryption in transit and at rest, access limited to staff who need it, two-factor sign-in for staff, audit logs of staff actions, regular security testing, a written security programme reviewed every year, and backups we test every month. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell the Data Protection Commission within 72 hours and tell affected users without delay.

## 8. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent. Most of this is self-service under Settings, Your data. Otherwise, write to [[Privacy email]]; we reply within 30 days. Students may also ask their teacher. You may complain to Ghana's Data Protection Commission ([dataprotection.org.gh](https://dataprotection.org.gh/)). If you live in the EU or UK, you may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

## 9. Children

The Service is used by children, and we protect them with extra care. The Children and Schools document explains exactly how.

## 10. Research

Research use of data happens only through a separate, opt-in study with ethics approval, explained in the Research Participation document.

## 11. Changes and contact
