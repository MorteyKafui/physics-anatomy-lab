# Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 (draft) · Effective [[Effective date]] · For review by UEW's legal office and a data-protection practitioner

**The short version.**

- Accounts are switched off. Nobody signs in, so we never ask for your name, email, age or a class code.
- Your lab work stays on your device. It never reaches our servers.
- Nothing is stored about you except anonymous daily counts of how the lab is used.
- We never sell data, never show ads, and never build marketing profiles.
- There is no AI help, so nothing you type is sent to an AI provider.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

[[Operator]] is the data controller, registered with Ghana's Data Protection Commission under number [[DPC registration number]]. Our Data Protection Supervisor is [[Data Protection Supervisor]], reachable at [[Privacy email]].

## 2. What we collect, and why

| Who you are | What we collect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Why                                                                    |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Anyone      | Your lab work, stored **only on your device**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | To run the lab, offline too                                            |
| Anyone      | Anonymous counts: an experiment opened, an attempt started, a step finished, a hint shown, a report exported or an attempt finished, with the experiment, step, mode, export format, kind of device (phone, tablet or desktop) and app version. Your device makes a random id that changes every day, so we can count visitors each day but cannot link one day's visit to the next. **No name, readings, free text, location or cookie.** Nothing is sent if your browser asks not to be tracked (Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control) | To see how many people use the lab and which experiments they use most |
| Everyone    | IP address and browser type, processed briefly by our hosting provider for security and abuse prevention; error reports with personal data removed                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | To keep the Service safe and working                                   |

**What we do not collect:** your name, email, age, location, contacts, camera or photos, or recordings of your voice. Spoken coaching is played to you; nothing you say is recorded in this version.

## 3. Our lawful bases

- **Legitimate interests:** counting how the lab is used, anonymously; security, preventing abuse, and fixing errors, always balanced against your rights.
- **Legal obligation:** keeping records the law requires.

## 4. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers who process it for us under written contracts, and only as much as each needs:

| Provider                    | What they do for us          | Data involved                                     |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Cloudflare                  | Hosting and security         | Every request passes through their network        |
| Neon                        | Database hosting             | The anonymous usage counts                        |
| [[Text-to-speech provider]] | Generating the coach's voice | Only our scripted lines, never your personal data |
| Sentry                      | Error reports                | Technical details, with personal data removed     |

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect a child or anyone else from serious harm (see the Children and Schools document). We never sell personal data.

## 5. Data stored outside Ghana

Our providers store and process data in data centres outside Ghana, including in Europe and the United States. We use providers that commit to strong security and to contract terms that protect your data to the standard Ghanaian and European law expects.

## 6. How long we keep data

| Data                   | Kept for                                                 |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lab work               | On your device until you delete it; never on our servers |
| Anonymous usage counts | 13 months, then deleted                                  |
| Security logs          | 90 days                                                  |

## 7. How we protect it

Encryption in transit and at rest, access limited to staff who need it, two-factor sign-in for staff, audit logs of staff actions, regular security testing, a written security programme reviewed every year, and backups we test every month. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell the Data Protection Commission within 72 hours.

## 8. Your rights

We hold nothing that identifies you, so there is nothing of yours on our servers to see, correct or delete. Your work is on your device: you can export it or delete it there at any time. For any question, write to [[Privacy email]]; we reply within 30 days. You may complain to Ghana's Data Protection Commission ([dataprotection.org.gh](https://dataprotection.org.gh/)). If you live in the EU or UK, you may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

## 9. Children

The Service is used by children, and we protect them with extra care. No child gives us a name, an email, an age or a class code. The Children and Schools document explains how we protect them.

## 10. Research

Research use of data happens only through a separate, opt-in study with ethics approval, explained in the Research Participation document.
