# Harvest review: Physics Bench and Wavelab

Reviewed 6 November 2026, module by module, for decision D5 ("harvest, don't import"). Of the
65 entries below (one covers eight practicals), 27 are kept, 5 are kept for after v1, 24 are
rewritten and 9 are dropped. Nearly all of Physics Bench's engine is kept, along with Wavelab's
figure kit and wave physics. Every screen in both projects is dropped or rewritten, because the
approved mockups in `design/` now define the screens.

| Project       | Where                                          | Commit    | Unit tests                               |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Physics Bench | `Desktop/lvl-200-pp-sem-1/virtual-physics-lab` | `5ecb5af` | 103 pass, 0 fail (`core`, `experiments`) |
| Wavelab       | `Desktop/wave-motion`                          | `f54d509` | 240 pass, 0 fail (`src`)                 |

A plain `bun test` in either project also picks up the Playwright specs and reports them as
errors (3 in Physics Bench, 7 in Wavelab). Those are not failing unit tests.

## What the verdicts mean

- **Keep:** copy the code into the named package, with its tests, and review it line by line
  there. Nothing is imported from the old projects as a dependency.
- **Keep, after v1:** sound and tested, but its experiment or topic is not one of the four v1
  flagships. It stays where it is until that topic is planned.
- **Rewrite:** the idea is kept, but the code is written fresh against our domain model, the
  approved mockups or a decision record. The reason says what changes.
- **Drop:** not carried over.

A physics module that is kept still follows AGENTS.md rule 1. It needs tests against the
analytic result, an independent model in `validation/`, and a physics reviewer's approval.
