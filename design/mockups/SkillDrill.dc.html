Before your first reading

Practise the skill first

A short drill, the first time you use each instrument. It takes about a minute and is not marked.
  1. Find what one small division is worth. From 0 to 3 V there are 60 divisions, so each is 3 ÷ 60 = 0.05 V.
  2. Start at the last numbered mark before the needle and count the divisions after it.
  3. Look square-on: the needle should hide its image in the mirror strip.
