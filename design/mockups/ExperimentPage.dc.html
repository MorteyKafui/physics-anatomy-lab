Simple pendulum

To determine the acceleration due to gravity, g, using a simple pendulum.

Open the lab Check your own lab data Print sheets Exam mode
Free, no sign-up. Works offline once opened.
The experiment card's isometric stage, from the landing page

What you do

  1. Build the stand: base, rod, boss and clamp, split cork, thread, bob.
  2. Measure l from the cork to the centre of the bob.
  3. Time 20 swings from the mark, counting from zero.
  4. Repeat at six lengths, and tabulate.
  5. Plot T² against l, and find g from the slope.

What the coach catches

Apparatus

  • A retort stand with a boss and clamp
  • A split cork, a thread and a bob
  • A digital stopwatch reading to 0.01 s
  • A metre rule
The report's apparatus list

The physics

T² = 4π² l ⁄ g

A graph of T² against l is a straight line through the origin. Its slope is 4π² ⁄ g, so g = 4π² ⁄ slope.

Model card (decision 3)

  • Equations: the exact period, by the arithmetic–geometric mean.
  • Assumes: a point bob on a light thread that does not stretch; no air drag.
  • Limits: swings up to 60°; below 10° the small-angle formula is within 0.2%.
  • Validated: against SciPy's elliptic integral, to 1 part in 10¹².
