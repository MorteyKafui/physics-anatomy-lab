Why the period depends on length Gravity pulls the bob back toward the middle. For small swings the pull grows in step with the distance from the middle, which is exactly the condition for a steady, repeating swing.

Square both sides: T² = (4π² ⁄ g) l. A graph of T² against l is a straight line through the origin, and its slope is 4π² ⁄ g.
Precautions examiners look for
  • I kept the angle of swing small, less than 10°.
  • I timed from the centre mark, where the bob moves fastest.
  • I clamped the thread between split corks so the length stayed fixed.
  • I repeated each timing and used the mean.
  • I made sure the bob swung in a plane, not in a circle.
