Question 1 · Simple pendulum · 60 minutes

You are provided with a retort stand, a split cork, a thread, a bob and a stopwatch.

  1. Set up the apparatus as shown, with the thread clamped in the split cork. Draw the set-up and label it.
  2. Displace the bob through a small angle and time 20 oscillations for l = 0.600 m. Record the time t.
  3. Repeat for l = 0.700, 0.800, 0.900 and 1.000 m. Find the period T and T² for each length. Tabulate your readings.
  4. Plot a graph of T² on the vertical axis against l on the horizontal axis. Determine the slope s of the graph.
  5. Calculate g from g = 4π² ⁄ s. State two precautions you took.
  6. Part (b): answer the questions that follow the experiment.
