How a practical runs

The same ten steps in every practical, in the order the real one takes. The coach speaks at each step, and goes quiet in exam mode.

  1. 1QuestionWhat are you finding? Here, g.
  2. 6TabulateBuild your own table, units in the headings.
  3. 2PredictGuess how T changes with l before you measure.
  4. 7PlotT² against l, on scales you choose.
  5. 3BuildPut the stand together, part by part.
  6. 8AnalyseFind the slope, then g.
  7. 4CheckThe coach checks every joint and length.
  8. 9ConcludeSay what you found and how sure you are.
  9. 5MeasureTime 20 swings at each length.
  10. 10ReportDownload it as a PDF, ready to hand in.

How it behaves

  • The bob swings at the period the lab computes for l = 0.800 m. Drag it to change the swing; the readings and the coach's note follow.
  • Point at or tab to a numbered part: its note opens beside the number (Plate I shows part 6).
  • The tabs are the list of practicals and work from the first screen. The open tab joins the plate.
  • Reduced motion and Lite mode: the plate is still, with the same readings.
  • One red-orange action per screen. Topic hues appear only on tab and plate markers.
  • No sign-in, sign-up or class code anywhere (ADR 0047).
