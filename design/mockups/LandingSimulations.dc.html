Not every question needs a practical. Open a simulation, move anything, and watch the physics answer: on apparatus that looks like the real thing, with the sound it really makes.

  • Real apparatusA sonometer, a resonance tube, a flask, drawn part by part as you'd find them in a school lab.
  • See the invisibleSlow time down a thousand times and watch the standing wave on a wire, or the air moving in a tube.
  • Hear itEvery change sounds as it would in the lab: the note climbs as the wire tightens.
  • Nothing markedNo steps and no score. When you want the marked version, the practicals are a click away.
