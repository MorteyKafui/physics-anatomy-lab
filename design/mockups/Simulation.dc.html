Try this

  1. Choose the 512 Hz fork. Where must the bridge go now? Predict, then slide it.
  2. Make the load four times as big. What happens to the note?
  3. Find the length where the 256 Hz fork makes the wire vibrate in two loops.

Each one checks itself when you get there, and says why. Nothing is marked.

Why it happens

A wave runs along the wire, reflects at each bridge and meets itself. Only waves that fit a whole number of half-wavelengths between the bridges survive: that standing wave is the note you hear. A tighter or lighter wire carries waves faster, so the note goes up.
